# Divisibility and Congruence

It is okay if the notation takes a pass or two to settle. Read what you can; these ideas return constantly in later arguments.

## Divisibility

**a | b**: a divides b — there exists an integer k with b = a·k.

**a ∤ b**: a does not divide b.

Examples: 3 | 6, 17 | 68, 4 ∤ 9.

## Congruence

For integers a, b and natural number n,

**a ≡ b (mod n)** means n | (a − b): a and b leave the same remainder when divided by n.

In this lecture this is often read “a equals b modulo n.”

Examples: 17 ≡ 32 (mod 5), 15 ≡ 29 (mod 7).
