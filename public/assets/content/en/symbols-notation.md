# Symbols and Notation

It is okay if some of these shortcuts feel opaque at first. Read what you can; you will see them reused until they feel ordinary.

Shortcuts used throughout the lecture.

## Quantifiers

**∀**: For all.

**∃**: There exists.

## Proof abbreviations

**S.T.**: Such that.

**WTS**: Want to show.

**ETS**: Enough to show.

These let you compress statements like Fermat’s Last Theorem: for all integers n ≥ 3, there do not exist nonzero integers x, y, z such that xⁿ + yⁿ = zⁿ.
